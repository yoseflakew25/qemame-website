Who we are

A coffee export company
out of Addis Ababa.

QEMAME TRADING PLC is a new and ambitious coffee export company based in Ethiopia, prepared to supply high quality green coffee beans, both specialty and commercial, from Ethiopia's highlands to the international market.

We take pride in providing exceptional quality and flavour by sourcing our beans from Ethiopia's well known highlands, particularly the resource rich Bench Maji region in the south west, home to the world famous Geisha coffee.

The company was founded in 2025 and works out of Addis Ababa, the city where Ethiopia's coffee trade meets the road to the port. Being new means we have no old habits to defend: every relationship we build, with a grower or with a buyer, is one we intend to keep.

Our mission

At QEMAME, we strive to bridge the gap between Ethiopia's best coffee growers and the worldwide market by providing high quality, ethically sourced coffee with integrity and excellence. We are committed to empowering local farmers, promoting sustainable methods and highlighting Ethiopia's rich coffee legacy to the world, one cup at a time.

Our vision

To empower Ethiopian farmers, provide high quality coffee worldwide, and promote sustainable commerce that benefits communities and preserves our rich heritage.

Bench Maji, south west Ethiopia

Where Geisha comes from.

The variety the world now calls Geisha was collected from a forest in south west Ethiopia in the 1930s. It travelled to Kenya, to Tanzania, to Costa Rica, and became the most talked about coffee on earth once it was replanted in Panama. Its home never moved. It is still here, in the forests of Bench Maji, and it is one of the reasons we source where we source.

The legend of Kaldi · Kaffa forest, around the ninth century

01

In the wild forest of Kaffa, where coffee still grows unplanted under the canopy, a young goat herder called Kaldi walked his flock home at dusk.

02

One evening the goats refused to sleep. They had eaten the crimson cherries of a low, glossy tree, and they danced until the stars turned.

03

Kaldi carried the cherries to a monastery. A monk cast them into the fire, then rescued the fragrant beans, ground them, and steeped them in water. The prayers ran all night.

04

From that fire the drink crossed to Yemen, Cairo, Venice, Vienna, and every city with a window and a chair. It began here. It still begins here.

Yirgacheffe

Gedeo Zone, SNNPR

2,200metres, top of range

The name every filter drinker already knows. Cool nights on the Gedeo escarpment slow the cherry down until the sugars are dense, and the washed lots come out of the cup like tea with flowers in it. If a buyer has only ever had one Ethiopian coffee, it was probably this one.

  • HarvestNovember to January
  • ProcessWashed and natural
